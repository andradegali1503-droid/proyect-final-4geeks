# UI/UX Pro Max

## Mission
Ensure every touchpoint in the guest journey feels intuitive, premium, and frictionless.

## Guidelines
1. **Clear Visual Hierarchy**: Lead the eye naturally to high-intent actions (Reserve a Table, Explore Menu, Confirm Booking).
2. **Predictable Feedback**: Provide immediate, elegant visual and communicative feedback for form entries, date selections, and loading states.
3. **Information Density with Breathing Room**: Avoid crowding while ensuring key details (time slots, dietary restrictions, party size) are immediately scannable.
4. **Error Prevention & Recovery**: Clear inline validation with helpful, polite error states matching hospitality tone.
