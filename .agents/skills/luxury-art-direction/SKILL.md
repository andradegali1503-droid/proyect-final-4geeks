# Luxury Art Direction — 5-Star Hospitality

## Visual Identity
1. **Color Palette**:
   - Deep background tones: Velvet Obsidian (`#0B0E14`), Deep Charcoal (`#131722`), Soft Slate (`#1E2333`).
   - Accents: Warm Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#C5A059`), Polished Bronze (`#A3704C`), Crisp Muted Ivory (`#F5F5F0`).
   - Feedback: Subdued Emerald (`#2E7D32`), Elegant Bordeaux/Crimson (`#8B0000`).
2. **Typography**:
   - Display/Headings: High-contrast Editorial Serif (Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond style) communicating heritage and exclusivity.
   - Body & UI: Clean, legible geometric or humanist Sans-Serif (Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Montserrat) with balanced letter-spacing.
3. **Atmospheric Depth**:
   - Subtle radial gradients, glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur`), delicate 1px borders with low-opacity metallics (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
   - High-fidelity culinary and ambiance imagery with rich shadow detail.
