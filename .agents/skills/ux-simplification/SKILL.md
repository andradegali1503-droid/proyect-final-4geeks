# UX Simplification

## Philosophy
Luxury is effortless. Every unnecessary click, ambiguous input, or crowded UI element degrades the perception of premium quality.

## Guidelines
1. **Linear Reservation Journey**:
   - Step 1: Party Size & Date.
   - Step 2: Time Slot & Seating Area (Main Dining Room, Chef's Table, Terrace).
   - Step 3: Guest Details & Special Requests.
   - Step 4: Instant Confirmation & Calendar/Email trigger.
2. **Reduced Cognitive Load**:
   - Limit choices per view; surface recommended or prime slots prominently.
   - Group related fields cohesively.
   - Replace cluttered tables and chaotic forms with clean step indicators and structured summaries.
